# Financial arithmetic and dependency fixes — 29 October 2026

- Server, browser offline projection and report journal now share exact BigInt largest-remainder allocation of integer cents. Remainder ties follow the original item order. For item totals 1/10/100 taka and payment 37 taka, shares are 0.34/3.33/33.33, with no floating-point tie drift. Gross lines and invoice subtotal use the existing 1e12-taka monetary bound; oversized calculations fail before allocation. Existing stored transactions are not rewritten.
- Flutter uses the same exact algorithm, rejects oversized/non-finite inputs, normalizes rates before multiplying, and orders synced invoice items by sale_id before projecting returns. Its original 64-bit total*weight intermediate could overflow and produce an enormous residual loop. Allocation now uses BigInt quotients/remainders and a bounded remainder loop. Calculator payment actions show validation failures instead of throwing into the UI.
- The 33 shared non-legacy invalid-input cases are rejected offline, including invalid enum values, mismatched order totals, malformed opening balances and non-finite amounts. Valid default bag weights and customer credits remain supported.
- Python reserves one of ten attempts under its mutex before password hashing. In-flight guesses count toward the limit. A deterministic 40-thread test admits exactly 10 hashes/401 responses and rejects 30 requests with 429; anonymous challenges do not consume or reset attempts.
